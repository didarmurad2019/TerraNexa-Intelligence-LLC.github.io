# Bylaws of TerraNexa Intelligence, LLC
*Effective Date: [Date] · Adopted by the Members and Managers of the Company*

## Article I – Name, Purpose and Offices
**1.1 Name.** The name of the company is TerraNexa Intelligence, LLC (the "Company"), a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of [State].
**1.2 Purpose.** The Company is organized to develop and provide geospatial, environmental and data-intelligence products and services, and to engage in any lawful business permitted under applicable law.
**1.3 Principal Office.** The principal office is at [Address]. The Managers may change it or establish other offices at any time.
**1.4 Registered Agent.** The Company shall maintain a registered agent and office as required by law.
**1.5 Governing Documents.** These Bylaws operate together with the Articles of Organization and any Operating Agreement. If they conflict, the Articles of Organization control, then the Operating Agreement, then these Bylaws.

## Article II – Members
**2.1 Membership.** Members are the persons admitted to the Company and listed in the Company records, each holding a Membership Interest.
**2.2 Admission.** New Members may be admitted only with the approval of Members holding a majority of Membership Interests and upon signing a joinder to these Bylaws.
**2.3 Meetings.** An annual meeting shall be held each year. Special meetings may be called by any Manager or by Members holding at least twenty percent (20%) of Membership Interests.
**2.4 Notice.** Written notice, including by email, stating the time, place and purpose shall be given at least ten (10) and no more than sixty (60) days before a meeting.
**2.5 Quorum and Voting.** Members holding a majority of Membership Interests constitute a quorum. Unless stated otherwise, a majority of Interests present decides a matter. Members may attend remotely and vote by proxy.
**2.6 Action Without Meeting.** Any action may be taken without a meeting by written consent of Members holding the votes needed to approve it.
**2.7 Major Decisions.** The following require approval of Members holding at least two-thirds (2/3) of Interests:
- Sale of all or substantially all assets, merger, conversion or dissolution
- Amendment of the Articles of Organization or these Bylaws
- Issuance of new Membership Interests
- Incurring debt above $[Amount] outside the approved budget

## Article III – Management
**3.1 Manager-Managed.** The business is managed by one or more Managers elected by the Members. Managers need not be Members.
**3.2 Authority.** Subject to Section 2.7, Managers have full authority to conduct the Company's ordinary business and to bind the Company.
**3.3 Number, Term and Election.** The initial number of Managers is [Number]. Managers serve until their successors are elected, or until earlier death, resignation or removal.
**3.4 Removal and Vacancies.** Members holding a majority of Interests may remove a Manager with or without cause. Vacancies are filled by vote of the Members.
**3.5 Manager Meetings.** Managers shall meet at least quarterly. A majority of Managers constitutes a quorum and decides by majority vote.
**3.6 Compensation.** Managers may receive reasonable compensation approved by the Members, plus reimbursement of reasonable expenses.

## Article IV – Officers
**4.1 Officers.** The Managers may appoint a Chief Executive Officer, a Chief Technology Officer, a Secretary, a Treasurer and other officers.
**4.2 Duties.** The CEO leads day-to-day operations. The Secretary keeps minutes and records. The Treasurer oversees funds, books and financial reporting.
**4.3 Removal.** Any officer may be removed by the Managers at any time, without prejudice to contract rights.

## Article V – Capital, Distributions and Finance
**5.1 Capital Contributions.** Contributions are recorded in the Company records. No Member must make additional contributions without their consent.
**5.2 Distributions.** Distributions are made to Members in proportion to their Membership Interests at times set by the Managers, subject to law and reasonable reserves.
**5.3 Tax Treatment.** Unless the Members elect otherwise, the Company is taxed as provided in its Operating Agreement. Allocations follow the Internal Revenue Code.
**5.4 Books and Records.** The Company keeps complete books at its principal office. Members may inspect them on reasonable notice for a proper purpose.
**5.5 Fiscal Year and Banking.** The fiscal year is the calendar year. Funds are held only in accounts approved by the Managers.

## Article VI – Transfers of Interests
**6.1 Restrictions.** No Member may sell, assign or encumber an Interest without the consent of Members holding a majority of the other Interests.
**6.2 Right of First Refusal.** Before any permitted transfer to a third party, the Company, then the remaining Members, may buy the Interest on the same terms within thirty (30) days.
**6.3 Events of Withdrawal.** Death, disability, bankruptcy or withdrawal of a Member does not dissolve the Company. The Company or the Members may purchase the Interest at fair market value.

## Article VII – Data Governance, Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
**7.1 Confidentiality.** Members, Managers, officers and personnel shall protect confidential information, including source code, models, datasets and client data.
**7.2 Data Protection.** The Company shall handle personal and client data under applicable privacy and security laws and maintain reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.
**7.3 Responsible Technology.** The Company commits to lawful, transparent and ethical use of data and analytics, including review of material AI and geospatial products for accuracy, bias and misuse risk.
**7.4 Intellectual Property.** Work product created for the Company belongs to the Company. Contributors shall sign appropriate assignment agreements.
**7.5 Conflicts of Interest.** Insiders shall disclose any personal interest in a Company transaction. Such transactions require approval of disinterested Managers or Members.

## Article VIII – Liability and Indemnification
**8.1 Limited Liability.** Members, Managers and officers are not personally liable for Company debts solely by reason of that role.
**8.2 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify any person acting in good faith for the Company against losses and expenses, except for fraud, willful misconduct, gross negligence or knowing violation of law.
**8.3 Advancement and Insurance.** The Company may advance expenses on receipt of an undertaking to repay, and may maintain liability insurance.

## Article IX – Dissolution
**9.1 Events.** The Company dissolves upon Member approval under Section 2.7, judicial decree, or any event requiring dissolution by law.
**9.2 Winding Up.** Assets are applied first to creditors, then to reserves for liabilities, then to Members in proportion to their Interests.

## Article X – General Provisions
**10.1 Amendments.** These Bylaws may be amended as provided in Section 2.7.
**10.2 Dispute Resolution.** Disputes shall first go to good-faith negotiation, then mediation, then binding arbitration or the courts of [State/County], as agreed.
**10.3 Electronic Records and Signatures.** Electronic notices, records and signatures are valid.
**10.4 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the rest remain in effect.
**10.5 Governing Law.** These Bylaws are governed by the laws of the State of [State].
